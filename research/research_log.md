## 3. Early computational reconnaissance

## 4. Derivation of the main results

* **Bottleneck bound.**  Derived and proved (Theorem 1).  Reduced to
  $\lambda_2\ge 20/7>2\sqrt2$ whenever the smaller of two arms is $\ge7$.
  Numerically confirmed the bottleneck bound is *not* sufficient when the two
  smaller arms both have order $5$: the bound gives $2.800 < 2\sqrt2$.
* **Enriching the test space.**  Allowing $x_z\neq0$ (using
  $\mathbf 1_{C_i}-n_ie_z$) and adding tip-dip vectors $q_i$ raised the bound
  to $2.8508$ at $(5,5,5)$, still $>2\sqrt2$ but the margin is thin ($0.0224$),
  and the 6-dimensional space was needed to cover all $\ell$.
* **Minimising the case analysis.**  Realised the bottleneck bound alone
  reduces everything to the single family $(5,5,\ell)$ — this is what made the
  symbolic computation possible.  (Corollary 1.)
* **Symbolic pencil.**  Three failed hand derivations before the correct
  formula was found; the check that caught them was "the computed top
  eigenvalue exceeded $3$, impossible for a cubic graph".  Recorded as B2 in
  `research/proof_attempts.md`.
* **Symmetric analysis of $M_\ell-2\sqrt2K_\ell$.**  The $(P,R)$ principal
  minor is $-4(\ell+11)((14\sqrt2-19)\ell-5\sqrt2+2)$, negative for every
  $\ell\ge7$ (threshold $\approx6.347$).  The single remaining value $\ell=5$ is
  settled by the rational witness $(-3/5,1/5,6/5,-2/5)$ with exact gap
  $(19584-13740\sqrt2)/625>0$.

## 6. B5 (further correction found by the audit)

While writing `mathematics/definitions.md` I initially wrote that switching
"leaves every vertex sum unchanged" in the module docstring of
`src/signings/core.py`.  The test suite caught the same error a second time
(this docstring, not the test).  The docstring was corrected.  The correct
statement is: switching preserves the spectrum; vertex sums become
$\sigma'(v)=\delta(v)\sum_{w\sim v}\sigma(\{v,w\})\delta(w)$.
